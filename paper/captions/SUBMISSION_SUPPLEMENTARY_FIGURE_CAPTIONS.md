# Journal of Hydrology supplementary figure captions

## Supplementary Figure S1

**Representative water-demand series before and after data cleaning.** **a–b**, Raw and cleaned demand, respectively, for DMA F, which had the highest missing-data rate among the ten DMAs. The displayed 168 h interval is selected by maximizing the number of missing observations while retaining at least half of the original hourly measurements. Orange ticks in **a** indicate missing timestamps. **c–d**, Raw and cleaned demand for DMA C, which had the highest number of observations outside the training-derived Tukey 1.5-IQR limits. Orange crosses in **c** identify the affected observations. Missing values and identified outliers are interpolated independently within the training and test periods; paired panels use identical vertical scales.

## Supplementary Figure S2

**Weekly water-demand patterns across representative DMAs.** Hour-of-week median demand is shown for **a**, DMA C; **b**, DMA H; and **c**, DMA E, representing the lowest, closest-to-median and highest mean training-period demand, respectively. Shaded bands denote the interquartile range across training weeks, vertical lines separate consecutive days and the gray background identifies the weekend. Demand is expressed in L s⁻¹.

## Supplementary Figure S3

**Empirical distributions of forecast-origin MAE.** Empirical cumulative distribution functions of per-origin MAE are shown for **a**, 24 h and **b**, 168 h forecasting. Each curve contains the same 46 common test origins for DCRNN, STGCN and STaR-GNN. MAE is the sum of the ten DMA-level MAEs, and a leftward curve indicates lower errors across the forecast-origin population.
